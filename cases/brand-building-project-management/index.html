Summary

  • N(Y)OOMOON, a Copenhagen based FemTech startup, wanted to plan a marketing campaign, analyse their business processes and improve their inbound sales.
  • Deranged’s RevOps team were hired to provide consulting services on best practices in marketing, evaluate N(Y)OOMOON’s business workflows and suggest improvements.
  • The initial phase focused on preparing a marketing campaign to help define their ideal client profile and build on brand awareness.
  • A use case for the project management tool, Trello, was found, and agile project management practices were introduced to N(Y)OOMOON.
  • Ongoing consulting support for brand awareness campaigns
