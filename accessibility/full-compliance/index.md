## Full compliance in collaboration with leading experts

Achieving legal accessibility compliance often requires more than just clean code and effective workflows. You also need formal documentation, manual audits, and real-world validation from end users.

We partner with the industry’s top specialists in auditing, testing, and accessibility training. This means deranged can deliver the entire spectrum of accessibility services—acting as your dedicated technical partner to ensure test results are immediately translated into actionable code and process improvements.

### With deranged managing your accessibility initiative, you gain seamless access to:

- **Specialized accessibility audits:** Thorough manual expert audits conducted against EN 301 549, WCAG 2.1, and WCAG 2.2 standards to ensure full compliance.
- **End-user testing:** Usability testing performed by individuals with diverse disabilities to validate real-world accessibility and practical usability.
- **Accessibility statements:** Professional assistance in drafting and maintaining statutory accessibility statements required by law.
- **Workshops & training:** Tailored presentations and training courses designed for your team—whether for content editors, UX designers, or key decision-makers.

By letting us orchestrate the entire process, you ensure that audit reports never collect dust — they are immediately turned into concrete improvements in your codebase and development workflows.
