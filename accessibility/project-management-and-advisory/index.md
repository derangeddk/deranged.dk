## Project management & advisory for complex initiatives

Large accessibility initiatives often stall due to intricate legal jargon, overwhelming audit reports, and ambiguous ownership. At deranged, we guide organizations navigating complex software architectures and organizational workflows to achieve accessibility safely and efficiently.

We take charge of your accessibility projects end-to-end, ensuring progress is driven effectively and structurally across your entire organization.

### How we help:

- **Prioritization & roadmap:** We break down extensive compliance mandates into a clear, realistic action plan—ensuring your budget is directed where it delivers the highest impact.
- **Translating requirements:** We translate regulations (such as the European Accessibility Act and Web Accessibility Directive) and technical standards (EN 301 549 / WCAG) into concrete, actionable engineering tasks.
- **Cross-functional coordination:** We bridge the gap between business stakeholders, UX designers, internal software engineers, and external vendors.
- **Ongoing sparring & guidance:** We address day-to-day questions as they arise, preventing momentum from stalling due to ambiguity.

With deranged leading your project, accessibility shifts from a burdensome, unpredictable add-on to a well-managed and reliable process.
