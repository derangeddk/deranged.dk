# Expert Accessibility Evaluation
Have an expert test whether you comply with the requirements in EN 301 549, including WCAG 2.1 and WCAG 2.2, and thereby whether you meet the legal requirements of the European Accessibility Act (EAA) or the Web Accessibility Directive (WAD).

Whether your goal is to remediate your digital solution to create a more inclusive user experience or simply to document compliance (or lack thereof), an accessibility test is a good first step.

We recommend testing as early as possible in the process, and we can identify and prevent many accessibility issues already when a UX designer has made the first sketch, wireframe, or prototype, or when a developer has coded a single component. The earlier the issues are discovered, the cheaper and more rewarding the accessibility effort becomes.

## Our accessibility consultant performs evaluations of:
- Sketches, wireframes, and prototypes
- Components
- Websites
- Apps

When delivering the results of an accessibility test, we prefer to do the primary handover in a cross-disciplinary workshop. When project managers/webmasters/POs/PMs, UX designers, and developers sit together around the table, there is an opportunity not only to create an understanding of the issues but also to collaboratively find good, realistic solutions that are only a text adjustment or a few lines of code away from being implemented in practice.  

If you need a full compliance report to document your accessibility efforts, this can be purchased as an add-on.  
We are also happy to help translate the results of the test into content for an accessibility statement.
