## Consulting on your unique accessibility challenges
We offer accessibility consulting and sparring for both organizations and individual teams at the strategic, tactical, and operational level.

Consulting and sparring can also be offered to your external suppliers, so you get maximum value out of the hours they put into working with accessibility in your solutions.

For example, we can help with:

- Strategic sparring and planning of accessibility efforts in your organization.  
- Workshops and sparring with, for example, UX designers or developers on solving specific accessibility issues.  
- Guidance on what to do with reports, e.g., a report from an automated testing tool (Siteimprove, Monsido, Silktide, QualWeb, Wave, axe-core, or others) or a monitoring report from the Danish Agency for Digital Government.  
- Consulting on creating or updating an accessibility statement.
