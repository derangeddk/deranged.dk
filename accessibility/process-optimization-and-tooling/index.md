## Process optimization, tooling & upskilling

Accessibility shouldn't be handled as an afterthought every time a new version is released. It needs to be solved structurally. We help transform how your organization approaches accessibility, turning it into a seamless, natural part of your daily workflows.

We evaluate your entire product development pipeline—from initial design wireframes to production deployments—strengthening your processes, automation, and internal expertise.

### Our three focus areas:

- **Process integration:** We embed accessibility checks directly into your workflows (e.g., during UX design, backlog grooming, and Definition of Done) to catch issues as early as possible.
- **Tooling & automation:** We guide you in selecting, configuring, and integrating automated testing tools into your development environments and CI/CD pipelines, automatically catching structural defects and contrast issues during development.
- **Upskilling & coaching:** We equip your developers, designers, and product owners with best practices and hands-on knowledge, empowering your team to build accessible products independently moving forward.

By focusing on structure and infrastructure, we ensure you build lasting internal capability—saving your organization significant time and resources in the long run.
