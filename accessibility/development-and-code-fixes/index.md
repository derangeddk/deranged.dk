## Development and code fixes (Accessible by default)

Consulting and audit reports aren't enough on their own—the code needs fixing too. While traditional consultancies stop at handing over a report, we roll up our sleeves. As a dedicated software consultancy, we have the technical expertise to resolve accessibility issues directly in your source code.

Whether you need a brand-new digital solution built from scratch or have an existing platform burdened with technical accessibility debt, our engineering team gets you across the finish line.

### Our approach to engineering & code:

- **Accessible by default:** When building new websites, applications, or software components, we embed semantic HTML, ARIA roles, keyboard navigation, and color contrast into every feature from line one.
- **Remediation in existing codebases:** Sitting on an extensive defect list from an automated scan or a formal audit? We dive directly into your repositories to remediate the underlying issues.
- **Relieving internal team capacity:** If your in-house engineers are fully booked with business-critical features, we step in to handle accessibility remediation on your behalf.
- **Engineering sparring:** We also act as technical sparring partners for your internal developers, eliminating guesswork and guiding them toward correct, robust implementations.

We ensure your codebase doesn't just meet legal standards on paper, but delivers a seamless, barrier-free experience for all users in practice.
