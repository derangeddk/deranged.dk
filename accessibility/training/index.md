## Awareness and Skills Development in Digital Accessibility
We offer introductory courses and short talks on web accessibility to ensure that your entire department or team gains a basic understanding of what accessibility is.
We also provide advanced courses in digital accessibility for, among others:

- UX designers
- Web editors / content creators
- Developers
- Testers / QA
- Webmasters, project managers, Product Owners (PO), Product Managers (PM)
- etc.

In our training, we take our starting point in concrete examples that you can relate to — preferably from your own solution.
