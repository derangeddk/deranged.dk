What is digital accessibility?

➟ Digital accessibility is about creating inclusive digital experiences. It ensures that everyone can use your website or app seamlessly, regardless of impairments or disabilities. By eliminating digital barriers, you empower your users and drive better business outcomes.

Specific sectors are subject to strict legal accessibility requirements:

  • Web Accessibility Directive (WAD): Applies to the public sector, including municipalities, regional authorities, state agencies, and public bodies.
  • European Accessibility Act (EAA): Effective from June 28, 2025, applying to private sector entities such as banks, insurers, e-commerce, streaming services, and passenger transport.

If your organization is covered by these regulations, your digital products must meet the EN 301 549 harmonised standard (which incorporates WCAG 2.1 and WCAG 2.2 guidelines).

Our goal is to help you integrate accessibility into your everyday engineering culture—ensuring both full regulatory compliance and barrier-free user experiences.
