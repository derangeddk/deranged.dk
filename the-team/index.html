Anne Thyme Nørregaard

Staff Accessibility Consultant. M.Sc. IT (Cand.it), Digital Design and Communication

Anne is a Certified Professional in Web Accessibility (CPWA), the highest certification offered by the IAAP. With over 10 years experience in accessibility she has contributed to the development of automated testing tools, international standards, and accessibility training and upskilling for both public authorities, private organisations and their employees.

Since 2020, Anne has been a supplier to the Danish Agency for Digital Government, developing tools and methodologies to monitor and ensure public authorities’ websites and apps comply with the Danish implementation of the EU Web Accessibility Directive (webtilgængelighedsloven).
